CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------

The engineered-features Random Forest model achieved
a slightly better performance than the baseline model.

Accuracy increased from 66.30% to 66.75%.

The recall of the Highly Unproductive class improved
from 48% to 51%, which is particularly relevant to
the objective of detecting lost productive time.

The improvement is modest rather than dramatic.
This indicates that the original behavioral features
already captured a substantial amount of the useful
information in the dataset.

Further model evaluation and optimization will be
performed before selecting the final model.
