KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------------------------------------

1. The baseline Random Forest model achieved
   an accuracy of 66.30%.

2. The Focused class achieved the best performance
   with an F1-score of 0.75.

3. The Highly Unproductive class achieved an
   F1-score of 0.55 and recall of 0.48.

4. The confusion matrix shows that many
   Highly Unproductive sessions are classified
   as Distracted.

5. Activity Score is the most important baseline
   feature.

6. Switch Rate is the second most important
   feature.

7. The baseline model provides a reference point
   for evaluating the effectiveness of engineered
   features.

CONCLUSION
----------------------------------------------

The baseline model provides moderate predictive
performance with 66.30% accuracy. However, the
model has difficulty distinguishing Highly
Unproductive behavior from Distracted behavior.

Therefore, feature engineering and model
optimization will be performed in the next stage
to improve productivity-state detection.
