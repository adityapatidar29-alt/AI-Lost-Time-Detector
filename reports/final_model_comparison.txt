FINAL ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------

The engineered Random Forest achieved the highest
hold-out test accuracy of 66.75%.

However, the tuned Random Forest achieved the highest
recall for the Highly Unproductive class at 57%.

Since the primary objective of the AI Lost Time Detector
is to identify unproductive behavior, detection of the
Highly Unproductive class is an important evaluation
criterion.

The tuned Random Forest therefore provides a strong
candidate for the final model because it improves the
detection of highly unproductive sessions while
maintaining similar overall performance.

Final model selection will consider accuracy, macro F1,
class-wise performance, and the project's objective.
